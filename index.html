Can a strategy be 100%?

No — and anything claiming it is lying. This one wins about 36 trades in every 100. It makes money because the wins are bigger than the losses, not because it is right often. Expect long losing runs: 27 losses in a row is normal here, not a disaster.

"Ready for real money" can never mean "never loses". It means the bot did in practice what the tests said it would, for enough trades to trust it. That is the checklist above.
