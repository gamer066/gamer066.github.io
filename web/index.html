No. Hosting is free. The page lives on a free address. If you want your own name like yourshop.ae, you buy that name yourself (usually AED 50-100 a year) and I connect it.

I already have Instagram or Talabat. Do I still need this?

Keep both. Put this link in your Instagram bio so regular customers order from you directly, and you keep the whole amount instead of paying the app a cut.

What if I don't like it?

You see your finished page before you pay. If it is not right, I change it. If you still don't like it, you pay nothing.

Can I change prices myself?
