Can a strategy be 100%?

No — and anything claiming it is lying. This one wins about 36 trades in every 100. It makes money because the wins are bigger than the losses, not because it is right often. Long losing runs are normal here: 27 losses in a row is the middle case, not the disaster.

So "ready for real money" can never mean "never loses". It means each bot did in practice what the tests said it would, for enough trades to trust it. That is the checklist above.
